# Customer Retention & Cohort Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer retention, repeat purchase behavior, customer segmentation, and cohort performance using Power BI. The objective was to understand customer lifecycle trends, identify high-value customer segments, and uncover opportunities to improve customer retention and long-term revenue growth.

---

## Business Problem

Customer retention is one of the most important drivers of business growth. Acquiring new customers is often more expensive than retaining existing ones.

This project was developed to answer the following business questions:

* How effectively are customers being retained?
* Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
* How do customer cohorts behave over time?
* What opportunities exist to improve repeat purchases and customer lifetime value?

## Cohort & Retention Analysis

![Cohort Analysis](dashboard_overview%203.png)

### Retention Metrics

* Customer Retention Rate
* Repeat Customers
* Repeat Purchase Rate
* Cohort Performance Trends
* Customer Lifecycle Analysis

### Key Insights

* Repeat customers generated significantly higher revenue than one-time buyers.
* Certain customer cohorts maintained stronger retention rates over time.
* Retention performance gradually declined across later cohorts, highlighting opportunities for proactive customer engagement.

---

## Business Recommendations

### Improve Customer Retention

* Launch loyalty and rewards programs.
* Develop personalized customer engagement campaigns.
* Identify customers showing signs of churn and proactively re-engage them.

### Increase Repeat Purchases

* Implement post-purchase communication strategies.
* Offer personalized product recommendations.
* Introduce retention-focused promotions and incentives.

### Maximize Customer Value

* Focus marketing investment on high-value customer segments.
* Upsell loyal customers through personalized offers.
* Monitor cohort performance to identify emerging retention risks.
